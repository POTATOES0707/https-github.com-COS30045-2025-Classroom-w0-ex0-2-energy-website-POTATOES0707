TV Power Consumption Dashboard

Visualizing Energy Use and Price Trends in TVs

Star Rating vs Energy Consumption
This scatter plot shows the relationship between the energy efficiency star rating of TVs and their actual energy consumption. TVs with higher star ratings tend to consume less energy annually.
Electricity Spot Prices Over Time
This line chart presents average spot electricity prices in Australia over a series of years. It helps understand cost trends that influence TV operation expenses.
Mean Energy Consumption by Screen Technology
This bar chart compares the average energy consumption of TVs based on their screen technology. It highlights which technologies are more energy- efficient on average.
Energy Consumption Share by Screen Type
This donut chart illustrates the proportion of total energy use attributed to each screen type, providing a comparative view of their energy impact.
